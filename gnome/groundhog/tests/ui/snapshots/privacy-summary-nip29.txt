heading: Not end-to-end encrypted
encrypted: Messages are kept on groups.example without end-to-end encryption. Its operators can read every message.
what others can see:
- The operators of groups.example can read every message and see the member list, roles and when people post.
- groups.example sees your public key and IP address when you connect.
- Group members see what you write and your public key.
- Discovery relays can see which groups you look up.
what Groundhog can't protect:
- Message content: the relay's operators can read it.
- Membership: the relay knows everyone in the group.
- What members do with your messages: they can copy, forward or screenshot them.
- Timing: someone watching many relays at once may link when messages are sent and received.
- That you use Nostr, and which relays you use: they are visible to your network.
- Apps running as you on this computer outside a sandbox can read what Groundhog shows.
storage: Groundhog keeps a copy encrypted on this device. The relay keeps its own copy, which its operators can read.
